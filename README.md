# Hotel Revenue & Yield Optimization Dashboard

### Overview
A portfolio project designed to showcase commercial data analysis, pricing elasticity tracking, and revenue management modeling for multi-property hospitality operations. Built entirely in Microsoft Excel using structured data modeling principles.

### Key Metrics Tracked
- **Occupancy %**: Rooms sold vs. total inventory.
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate)**: Revenue generated per occupied room.
- **RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)**: The ultimate benchmark of hotel commercial performance.
- **CompSet Variance & Indexing**: Benchmarking internal room rates against regional competitor sets.
- **Seasonality & Demand Drivers**: Simulating ADR fluctuations during high-demand events (concerts, sports fixtures, conferences).

### File Structure
- `Dashboard`: Executive summary view highlighting high-level KPIs and monthly trends.
- `Hotel Data`: 730+ rows of simulated daily property data across multi-property locations.
- `Monthly Data`: Aggregated monthly performance summaries and rate index calculations.
- `Info`: Glossary of standard hospitality and revenue management metrics.

### Technical Skills Demonstrated
- Advanced Excel modeling & data structuring.
- KPI formula design (`RevPAR`, `ADR`, `Occupancy`).
- Market intelligence and competitor variance benchmarking.
